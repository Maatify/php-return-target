## Community Rules

- Treat people with respect in technical collaboration.
- Do not harass, threaten, personally attack, or discriminate against others.
- Do not dox people or deliberately disrupt project participation or services.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence, constraints, and
  implementation rather than people.

Maintainers may moderate or remove abusive content and may restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community and the project.

Private conduct concerns may be sent to `support@maatify.dev`.
